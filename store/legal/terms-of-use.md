## 1. What Prayan is

Prayan is a personal trading journal. You record trades you have already
decided to take, together with the rules you set for yourself, and Prayan
measures how closely you followed them. Its primary output is a discipline
score, not a profit figure.

- Prayan **does not predict returns** and makes no claim that following its

- Prayan **cannot place, route, modify or execute orders**, and has no

- Prayan **does not hold, transfer or custody money or assets**.
- Prayan is **not a broker, adviser, portfolio manager or research provider**,

Trading carries risk, including the loss of your entire capital. Every decision
you make about your money is yours alone. A high discipline score means you
followed your own rules; it says nothing about whether those rules are any good,
and it is not a prediction of anything.

## 3. Your account and your data

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter. Prayan calculates from
your figures; wrong inputs produce wrong scores.
