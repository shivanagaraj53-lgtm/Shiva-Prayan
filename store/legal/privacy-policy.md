# Privacy Policy — Prayan Trading Journal

**Last updated: 18 August 2026**

Prayan is a personal trading journal. This policy describes what the app does
with your information. It is written to be accurate about the version being
submitted, not aspirational — where a feature is not switched on yet, it says
so.

## The short version

- Your journal is stored **on your device**. In the version being submitted
  there is no server, and your trades never leave your phone.
- Prayan **never asks for your brokerage login**, and cannot place trades.
- Analytics is **off unless you turn it on**, and never includes what you
  traded, at what price, or how much money is involved.
- You can **export everything** and **delete your account and all its data**
  from inside the app.

## What the app stores

Everything below is written to your device's private app storage.

**Your account.** An email address and a password. The password is stored as a
derived value, never as the text you typed. This is used only to unlock the
journal on this device.

**Your journal.** The trades you enter — instrument, direction, entry, stop,
target, size, fees, times — plus the notes, tags, screenshots, emotional
check-ins and daily reviews you choose to record.

**Your configuration.** Your rules and their thresholds, your account currency
and starting equity, your strategies, your pre-trade checklist, your timezone,
your chosen theme and notification preferences.

**What Prayan derives from it.** Discipline scores, rule evaluations, streaks
and performance aggregates. These are calculations over what you entered; they
add no new information about you.

## What the app does not collect

- **No brokerage credentials.** Prayan has no broker connection and never asks
  for one.
- **No contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers.**
- **No background tracking.** The app does nothing when it is not open, except
  show the reminders you switch on.
- **No sale of data, ever.** Your journal is not a product we sell, share or
  license to anyone.

## Where your data lives

In the submitted version, on your device only. Prayan runs entirely offline.
Uninstalling the app removes it. Because it is local, we cannot recover your
password or your journal for you — see *Backups*, below.

**If you later enable cloud sync** (a future release, and always an explicit
choice), your journal would be stored in Google Firebase under an account only
you can read, and this policy will be updated and re-published before that
ships.

## Optional features that send data out

Both are **off by default** and each can be turned off again at any time in
Settings.

**Anonymous usage analytics.** If you switch this on, Prayan reports which
screens are used and whether features are working. Events deliberately carry no
instrument names, prices, position sizes, account balances or note text. The
purpose is to find broken flows, not to profile you.

**AI-written reflections.** If you switch this on, Prayan sends a computed
summary of a trading day to Anthropic's Claude API to be phrased into a written
reflection. What is sent is limited to: the day, your discipline score and rule
counts, the names of rules you broke, streak figures, the number of trades and
their win/loss split, a pre-formatted total for the day, and any mood tags you
recorded.

**What is never sent: the instruments you traded, any price, any position size,
and your account balance.** The service also rejects any reply that states a
number it was not given. If the feature is unavailable, Prayan writes the
reflection itself, on your device, from the same figures.

Anthropic processes this under its own terms as a service provider and does not
use it to train models. If you would rather nothing leave the device, leave this
setting off — every screen in the app works without it.

## Notifications

If you allow them, Prayan can remind you to complete your journal or your daily
review. It will not send notifications encouraging you to trade more, to trade
larger, or to open the app for its own sake.

## Your rights and controls

- **Export.** Settings → *Export my journal* writes everything you have logged
  to a file you keep.
- **Delete.** Settings → *Delete my account* removes your journal and your
  account from the device. Deletion means the data is gone, not that the login
  is disabled.
- **Withdraw consent.** Analytics and AI reflections can be switched off at any
  time, and take effect immediately.

If you are in a jurisdiction granting rights of access, correction, portability
or erasure — including the GDPR and India's DPDP Act — the export and delete
controls above satisfy them directly, and you can also write to
support@prayan.app.

## Backups

Your device's own backup system (iCloud Backup, Google Backup) may include the
app's data if you have that enabled. That copy is governed by Apple's or
Google's terms, not ours. We have no access to it.

## Children

Prayan is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Security

Data is held in your device's private app storage, protected by the operating
system's app sandbox and, if you enable it, your device lock and Prayan's own
biometric unlock. No system is perfectly secure, and a device that is unlocked,
rooted or jailbroken is outside what any app can protect.

## Not financial advice

Prayan is a journalling and reflection tool. It does not provide financial,
investment or trading advice, does not predict returns, does not recommend
instruments, and cannot place or execute orders. What it measures is whether you
followed rules you set for yourself. Decisions about your money remain entirely
your own.
